Privacy Policy

Data Check for Zoho CRM · Your data security matters

1. Introduction

This Privacy Policy describes how Data Check collects, uses, and protects data when you use our free Zoho CRM extension. We are committed to transparency and safeguarding your information.

2. Data We Access

Data Check scans your CRM records to identify quality issues. The following data categories are accessed solely for this purpose:

Data Category Purpose Modules
Record Fields Check for missing or incomplete data Leads, Contacts, Accounts
Email Addresses Identify missing or malformed emails Leads, Contacts
Phone Numbers Detect missing phone numbers Leads, Contacts, Accounts
Address Fields Find incomplete address information Leads, Contacts, Accounts
Account Details Check for missing account data Accounts

3. What We DON'T Access

  • Passwords
  • Financial data
  • Payment cards
  • Government IDs
  • Health data

4. Third-Party Services

Data Check is a free, standalone scanner and does not integrate with any third-party services for data processing. All scanning and analysis are performed locally within your Zoho CRM environment.

No external APIs · No data sharing

5. Data Storage

All data is stored within your Zoho CRM. Data Check does not maintain any external databases. You can delete data anytime by clearing fields or uninstalling the extension.
